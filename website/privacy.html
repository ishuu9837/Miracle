01 / THE SHORT VERSION

Your files.
Your computer.

Miracle’s desktop apps convert files locally. No Miracle account, cloud conversion service, advertising SDK, or automatic app telemetry is included in the current previews.

This policy describes the current Miracle desktop previews and this project’s website, maintained through ishuu9837/Miracle. The website and download hosts have their own data practices; local conversion does not mean that browsing a website creates no network requests.

02 / DESKTOP APPS

What stays on your device.

The app and its local engines read the files you select or drop, create temporary working files, and save results to your chosen location. Conversions do not upload your documents or media to a Miracle server. Files placed in a cloud-synced or network folder can still be transferred by that folder’s own service.

Mac preferences and history
Preferences are stored locally. When recent-job history is enabled, the Mac app keeps up to 50 entries containing the operation, source display name, status, and date in ~/Library/Application Support/Miracle/history.sqlite. Output URLs remain in memory for the session. Use Recent Jobs → Clear to remove entries, and turn off history in settings to stop recording new jobs. Removing the app alone may leave preferences and history behind.
Windows session data
The preview holds the selected paths, chosen output folder, and last result in memory. Conversion engines may create local temporary files and isolated profiles. Completed outputs remain until you delete them. Temporary work is cleaned up during normal job completion or cancellation; an interrupted process can leave local remnants.
Shortcuts and permissions
The Windows shortcut reads the selected file paths from foreground File Explorer. It observes Shift and Escape to control the wheel, without recording typed content or swallowing keyboard events. Turn it off in the app. Mac Finder integration uses the permissions requested by macOS; you can revoke them in System Settings. Revocation can disable the shortcut.
Sharing actions
If you explicitly use an OS sharing action such as AirDrop or copy a file to the clipboard, that action passes data to the recipient or operating system you choose. Other apps with clipboard access may read copied information.

OS crash reporting, antivirus inspection, backups, and sync tools are controlled by your system and their own settings. Miracle does not provide secure erasure of files or local history.

03 / THIS WEBSITE

A demo, not an upload.

The conversion wheel and walkthroughs use fictional sample files. They do not read or convert your personal files. We have not added analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or browser-storage identifiers to the site’s frontend. Animation state is held in the current page.

The site currently loads DM Sans and Space Grotesk through Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request the stylesheet and font files, which can expose connection information such as your IP address. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

The hosting service may process request details such as IP address, browser information, requested pages, and timestamps to deliver and secure the site. The current chatgpt.site preview is hosted through OpenAI Sites and can require host-managed sign-in; OpenAI’s Privacy Policy applies to that service. If you visit a separately deployed copy, its hosting provider’s policy applies instead. Miracle’s desktop apps do not require that website sign-in.

04 / DOWNLOADS & SUPPORT

Know where the link goes.

Downloads, source code, releases, and issue reports are hosted on GitHub. Visiting those links sends requests to GitHub, whose General Privacy Statement describes its processing.

Information you post in this public repository’s issues can be visible to everyone. Do not include private files, personal paths, secrets, or unredacted logs. Describe a problem with a small, nonsensitive example. We do not automatically receive app files, job histories, or diagnostic logs.

05 / YOUR CHOICES

You stay in control.

You can quit the tray or menu-bar app to stop its background shortcut, disable the Windows Explorer shortcut, revoke OS permissions, clear Mac history, and remove locally saved output files. Use your OS and browser settings to manage backups, site data, permissions, and third-party services.

For a privacy question, contact the maintainer through the project’s issue tracker using a nonsensitive description. Ask for a private contact method before sharing personal information. We will update this page when the described data practices change.

Effective and last updated: 19 October 2026. Scope: current Mac test build, Windows preview, and project website.
